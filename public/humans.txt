/* canary */
this site includes a pgp key canary at /canary. the live document is the
clearsigned statement at /canary.asc, not that page and not its rendered
HTML. verify it against the public key at /fiona.asc, and compare that
fingerprint against a source that is not this site — on a keyring that has
not certified this key, a good signature
reported alongside a warning that the key is not certified with a trusted
signature is the expected result, because only that fingerprint comparison can
tie the key to a person. if the signature fails or the fingerprint differs,
treat the page as tampered with. if no new statement appears by the renew-by
date, the old statement no longer speaks for today and nothing published here
should be relied on as current — though a missed renewal alone is not proof that
the key is compromised, because travel, illness, or a lost card will do it.
statements are signed by hand with a physical key; there is no automation and no
second operator. silence is the signal: do not read it as an oversight, and do
not go on trusting a stale statement.
